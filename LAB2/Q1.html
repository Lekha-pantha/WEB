Sports Week

Sport Week: Igniting Passion for Fitness and Fun Every year, our school buzzes with excitement during Sport Week—a seven-day celebration of athleticism, teamwork, and healthy competition. Kicking off with an energetic opening ceremony featuring parades, music, and a torch relay, the week unites students, teachers, and families in a whirlwind of activities.
