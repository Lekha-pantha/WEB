HTML Basics

Use of <p> tag

The <p> tag is used to create paragraphs of normal text.

The browser automatically adds space before and after each paragraph — you don’t need to add extra blank lines or <br> tags.

This is the second paragraph. Notice the clear space between this and the previous one — that’s the default behavior of the <p> tag.

Use of <pre> tag

The <pre> tag shows "preformatted" text. It keeps all spaces, tabs, and line breaks exactly as you typed them.
