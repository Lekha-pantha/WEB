Simple Search Bar (GET method)

After you type and click Search,
look at the URL — you will see ?q=your+search+words 

                :EXAMPLE:
        You type:  Best pizza Kathmandu
        After clicking Search,  the URL becomes something like:
        text https://www.google.com/search?q=best+pizza+Kathmandu


        ? → marks the start of the query string
        q= → "q" is the parameter name for the search terms
        best+pizza+Kathmandu → your words (spaces turned into +)
